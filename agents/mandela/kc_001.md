## Original Excerpt

> "During my lifetime I have dedicated myself to this struggle of the African people. I have fought against white domination, and I have fought against black domination. I have cherished the ideal of a democratic and free society in which all persons live together in harmony and with equal opportunities. It is an ideal which I hope to live for and to achieve. But if needs be, it is an ideal for which I am prepared to die."

## Summary

Mandela delivered this statement from the dock during the Rivonia Trial, where he and other ANC leaders faced the death penalty for sabotage. Instead of pleading for mercy, Mandela used the trial to articulate the moral foundations of the anti-apartheid struggle, framing it as a fight for universal human rights.

## Political Significance

- Established Mandela as a global symbol of resistance against oppression
- Articulated the core vision of a non-racial democracy
- Demonstrated his willingness to sacrifice for his principles
- Became one of the most quoted political statements of the 20th century
